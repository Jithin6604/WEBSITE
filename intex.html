COMMON QUESTIONS

Good questions deserve clear answers.

What is the platform?

It is a developing business analytics platform designed to help users prepare, explore, visualize, and understand business data.

Do I need to be a data analyst?

The goal is to make analytics more approachable for nontechnical business users. Some data preparation and interpretation may still be needed depending on the dataset.

What data can I use?

The current file-based workflow is intended for structured business data such as supported CSV and Excel datasets. Results depend on the quality and fields available in your data.

Does it connect to billing software automatically?

Automatic integrations are part of the longer-term direction. Do not assume a particular billing system is supported until its integration is available.

Are forecasts and AI recommendations available now?

These are future capabilities to validate and develop. Availability should be confirmed for the current product release.

How can my business participate?

Contact us to express interest in early access and share the business questions you want to solve.
